Started after Harvey hit home.

In 2017, Hurricane Harvey put Manny’s family and friends through what thousands of Houston households lived through: water where it never should have been, and no good answer for keeping it out next time.

The flood-prevention options on the market were ineffective, half-measures or priced out of reach. So Manny and the team designed and built their own drainage systems. That work became Flood Mitigators, and it is still family-owned and Houston-operated today.

FAQ

A few questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Tell us in the booking form and a specialist will reply, or call or text (281) 849-1082.

Can you assess my property remotely?
Yes. Every assessment we offer is remote. The Snapshot and Flood Intelligence report are built from your address, and the Expert Mitigation Plan is a 45-minute video call where you show us the problem areas on your phone.
Is the Flood Snapshot really free?
Yes. Enter your address and email and we send it. No card and no obligation. If it raises questions, the Flood Intelligence report answers them in detail.
How is Flood Intelligence different from the Snapshot?
The Snapshot tells you whether you have a problem. Flood Intelligence tells you why, with flood and rainfall history near your block, how water moves across your lot, and a ranked list of fixes.
What drainage problems do you help with?
Standing water in the yard, water pooling at the foundation, soggy side yards, overflowing gutters and downspouts, water reaching the garage or patio, and runoff coming from a neighbor's lot.
Will yard drainage stop street or bayou flooding?
Not always, and we'll tell you so. Drains handle the water that falls on and runs across your lot. If floodwater comes from the street or a bayou, your report covers what helps in that case and what won't.
What happens after the assessment?
You keep the report either way. If you want the work done, we turn your plan into a fixed install scope, schedule the crew, and handle the job from layout to cleanup.
Do you offer a warranty?
Yes. Drainage systems we install are backed by a 3-year parts and labor limited warranty. Your install scope spells out exactly what it covers.
What if water is coming in right now?
Call or text (281) 849-1082. We offer 24/7 emergency response. Regular office hours are Monday to Friday, 6 am to 6 pm Central.
What areas do you serve?
Greater Houston and the surrounding communities, from the Inner Loop out to Katy, Cypress, The Woodlands, Kingwood, Sugar Land and Pearland. Not sure about your street? Call or text and ask.
